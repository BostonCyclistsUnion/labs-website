# How is this going to be used?
BCU is advocating for a Safe Streets Ordinance, like Cambridge and Somerville have successfully implemented, to legally mandate a connected bike network be built in Boston on a timeline with the urgency of a city truly committed to achieving Vision Zero and preventing serious injuries and fatal collisions due to traffic violence. We are using this map to collect suggestions by the community on where you would find the most value for safer streets to help us refine what our ideal bike network would be.
